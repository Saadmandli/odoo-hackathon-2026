# VendorBridge — Demo Video Script (with voiceover)

**Target length:** ~4–5 minutes. Speak naturally; pause between scenes.
**Before recording:** open the app (npm run dev / your Vercel link), make the browser full-screen, and be logged out.
**Recording tools (free):** Windows **Game Bar** (press `Win + G`), or **Loom**, or **OBS Studio**.
**Format:** 🎙️ = what to SAY.  [SCREEN] = what to DO.

---

## Scene 1 — Intro (10s)
[SCREEN] Show the login page.
🎙️ "Hi, this is VendorBridge — a Procurement and Vendor Management ERP that digitizes how companies buy from suppliers, from request to payment, with built-in intelligence to make smarter buying decisions."

## Scene 2 — Login & roles (15s)
[SCREEN] Point at the four demo buttons. Click **Officer**, then **Sign in**.
🎙️ "The platform supports four roles — Procurement Officer, Vendor, Manager, and Admin — each with secure, role-based access. I'll start as a Procurement Officer."

## Scene 3 — Dashboard (20s)
[SCREEN] Let the dashboard load. Move the cursor across the cards.
🎙️ "This is the dashboard. At a glance we see active RFQs, pending approvals, total procurement spend, and — importantly — total money saved. Below are recent purchase orders and invoices, with quick actions to jump into any task."

## Scene 4 — Vendor Management (20s)
[SCREEN] Click **Vendors**. Scroll the list. Open the register form briefly, then close.
🎙️ "First, vendor management. We maintain organized supplier records — company details, GST numbers, categories, status, and ratings — with search and filtering. New vendors can be registered in seconds."

## Scene 5 — RFQ Creation (25s)
[SCREEN] Click **RFQs → New RFQ**. Fill a title like "Office Monitors", add an item, a budget, pick a deadline, attach a file, tick a couple of vendors. (You can submit, or just show it then cancel.)
🎙️ "To start buying, the officer creates an RFQ — a Request for Quotation. We add the items and quantities, set a budget and deadline, attach any specifications, and invite the suppliers we want to quote. They're notified instantly."

## Scene 6 — Smart Award Engine (40s)  ⭐ THE HIGHLIGHT
[SCREEN] Open RFQs → click **"Annual Stationery Supply"** (it has live competing quotes). Point at the Smart Award banner and the score column.
🎙️ "Here's what sets VendorBridge apart. When suppliers submit their quotes, our Smart Award engine scores each one out of a hundred — weighing price, delivery speed, vendor rating, and reliability. It doesn't just pick the cheapest; it recommends the best overall value and explains exactly why, in plain English. It even projects how much we'll save. This turns guesswork into defensible, data-driven sourcing."

## Scene 7 — Approval Workflow (30s)
[SCREEN] Open the AV Setup RFQ or go to **Approvals** (1 pending). Sign out, click **Manager**, sign in, open **Approvals**, click **Approve** with a remark.
🎙️ "Larger purchases need sign-off. VendorBridge routes approvals automatically — small orders auto-approve, bigger ones escalate to a Manager or Admin. As the Manager, I can approve or reject with remarks, and every decision is tracked on a timeline."

## Scene 8 — Purchase Order & Savings (25s)
[SCREEN] Sign back in as Officer. Go to **Purchase Orders**. Point at the Savings column and the Total Savings figure. Click **PDF** on a PO to show the document.
🎙️ "Once approved, an official Purchase Order is generated automatically — with tax and totals calculated, and the realized savings recorded. Every PO can be downloaded as a professional PDF."

## Scene 9 — Goods Receipt & 3-Way Match (25s)
[SCREEN] On a PO without goods, click **Receive**, confirm quantities. Then go to **Invoices**.
🎙️ "When the goods arrive, we record a Goods Receipt. This unlocks a powerful financial control — the three-way match. An invoice only clears when the Purchase Order, the goods received, and the invoice all agree."

## Scene 10 — Invoices (25s)
[SCREEN] On **Invoices**, point at the three match badges (Matched, Mismatch, Awaiting goods). Click **Download** on one, then **Email**.
🎙️ "Here are our invoices, each showing its match status — Matched, Mismatch, or Awaiting goods. This stops us ever paying for undelivered items. Invoices can be downloaded, printed, or emailed straight to the vendor as a PDF."

## Scene 11 — Reports & Analytics (30s)
[SCREEN] Click **Reports**. Show Total Savings, the monthly chart, spend-by-vendor, and scroll to vendor scorecards. Click **Export CSV**.
🎙️ "The Reports section turns all this into insight — total spend and savings, monthly trends, spend by vendor, and automatic vendor scorecards that grade each supplier from A to D on their performance. Everything can be exported to CSV."

## Scene 12 — Activity Log (10s)
[SCREEN] Click **Activity & Logs**. Show the audit trail and the notification bell.
🎙️ "And every action across the platform is captured in a full audit log, with real-time notifications."

## Scene 13 — Close (20s)
[SCREEN] Return to the Dashboard.
🎙️ "VendorBridge is built with Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL — a secure, production-grade full-stack ERP. It covers the entire procurement lifecycle and adds real intelligence on top: smart award scoring, automatic approvals, savings tracking, and three-way matching. Thank you for watching."

---

### Tips
- Speak a little slower than feels natural — it sounds clearer on playback.
- If you fumble, pause 2 seconds and repeat the line; you can trim it later.
- Total spoken time is ~4 minutes; add pauses and it lands around 5.
- Record in 1080p, browser full-screen (press F11) for a clean look.
